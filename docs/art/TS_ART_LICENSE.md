# Trash Stack — in-game art license

These are original game assets generated by **Cinder & Crown** with **Higgsfield** for *Trash Stack*, a 2D falling-block puzzle game. They are style-matched to the approved app-icon option A (neon city, glossy cartoon raccoon, dark background, magenta/cyan/lime neon).

- **Created:** 20 November 2026 (America/Chicago)
- **Studio:** Cinder & Crown (Cinder and Crown LLC)
- **Generator:** Higgsfield (Nicholas Will's connected Higgsfield workspace, Ultra plan)
- **Tooling:** Higgsfield MCP `generate_image_batch` (model `gpt_image_2_6`, shown in the catalog as GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst; job metadata also listed the internal model name `aurora-img-alpha`). Quality: medium for the board, tiles, and VFX; high for the raccoon poses. The board was generated at 2K 9:16 (1520×2688) and resized to 1080×1920. Tiles were generated at 1K and resized to 256×256. The slam burst was a 2-frame transparent GIF, then extracted to PNG with Python/Pillow. Higgsfield returned usable alpha on transparent assets, so no rembg pass was needed; zero-alpha pixels were cleaned locally.
- **Style reference:** the approved Trash Stack app-icon (bandit dumpster icon A, `docs/art/appicon-variant-a-1024.png`); earlier cute option-A is archived under `docs/art/archive/cute-appicon/`.
