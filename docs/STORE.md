# Trash Stack — store listing copy

**Name:** Trash Stack: Neon Junk Puzzle  
**Subtitle:** Recycling Night Shift  

## Description (short)

Work the neon junkyard night shift. Drop cans, bottles, pipes, and crates into an 8×14 dumpster, recycle matching materials, and slam the hydraulic press when your meter is ready. Fill order tickets, spend Scrap on your Junkyard, and climb the Season Pass — free to play with odds shown and no purchase nagging.

## Description (long)

Trash Stack is a material-matching dumpster game, not a block-letter puzzle.

- **Junk items** with materials (metal, plastic, glass, paper)
- **Recycle** clusters of 4+ matching materials into the chute
- **PRESS** — fill the meter, then crush the bottom rows into Scrap on your timing
- **Order tickets** every run for XP, Gems, and Pass progress
- **Snatch** once per run with your raccoon helper
- **Junkyard base**, Season Pass, Gem Shop, quests, and daily spin (odds always shown)

Free to play. Soft currency from play. Cosmetics and convenience only — never pay-to-win score. Age rating designed for 12+.
