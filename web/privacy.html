TRASH STACK

Privacy Policy · Cinder and Crown LLC

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Trash Stack is published by Cinder and Crown LLC. This policy applies to the Trash Stack mobile apps and the web version at trash-stack.pages.dev.

Data Not Collected

Trash Stack does not collect, share, sell, or transmit personal data. There is no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier use, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your device

Game progress (high score, cosmetics, settings, daily challenges) is stored only on your device using on-device storage (for example, Web Storage in the browser, or Capacitor Preferences / platform defaults on iOS and Android). That information never leaves your device through this app.

Networking

The mobile apps are designed to run fully offline. They do not require a network connection to play and do not make gameplay-related network requests.

Purchases

Version 1.0 does not offer in-app purchases. A “Premium Pass (coming soon)” control may appear in the UI but is disabled and does not process payments.

Children

Trash Stack is a casual puzzle game appropriate for ages 4+. Because we do not collect data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
