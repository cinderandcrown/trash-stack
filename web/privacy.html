TRASH STACK

Privacy Policy · Cinder and Crown LLC

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Trash Stack (App Store listing: Trash Stack: Neon Junk Puzzle) is published by Cinder and Crown LLC. This policy applies to the mobile apps and the web version at trash-stack.pages.dev.

Gameplay progress stays on-device. The game is a neon junk / compactor puzzle with optional AdMob ads (see below). ATT and UMP consent apply on iOS / GDPR regions.

Game progress (on device)

Scores, cosmetics, achievements, streaks, and settings are stored on your device (Web Storage / Capacitor Preferences / platform defaults). We do not operate player accounts.

Advertising

The free mobile apps are supported by Google AdMob ads:

  • Rewarded video — optional, for revive/continue, double Shinies after a run, or an extra daily spin
  • Interstitials — only between runs, capped (never in the first 2 runs of a session; at most one every 3 game overs)
  • No banners during play

AdMob may process advertising identifiers and ad interaction data under Google’s policies. On iOS we show App Tracking Transparency (ATT) before personalized tracking. We also present Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent where required (for example GDPR regions).

See Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

What we do not do

  • No real-money gambling or paid loot boxes
  • No sale of your personal information by Cinder and Crown LLC
  • Random rewards (daily spin, etc.) show odds in-app and are earned with soft currency or optional rewarded ads

Purchases (stubs in current build)

“Remove Ads” and “Premium Pass / Night Pass premium lane” may appear as disabled coming-soon controls. When enabled, they will use Apple/Google billing; this policy will be updated with product details.

Children

Trash Stack is a casual puzzle rated for a broad audience. Ads are subject to platform and AdMob child-directed / family policies as configured in AdMob. Parents can use device-level tracking and ad controls.
