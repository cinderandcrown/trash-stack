HOW TO PLAY

  1. Drop junk into the compactor bin (8×15). Full rows get crushed by the hydraulic press for scrap points.
  2. Match 3+ same-material tiles (metal, plastic, paper, glass) for a recycle pop bonus.
  3. Tap the bin to rotate. Swipe to move. Soft / hard drop as usual. DAS + lock delay kept snappy.
  4. Stash one piece in the stash bin. Spend a Magnet charge (Trash Panda’s trick) to yank the falling junk into stash.
  5. Junk looks like cans, boxes, tires, bags, pipes, and tubes — not plain bricks.
  6. Earn Scrap, build your Junkyard, level up, unlock looks. Rewarded ads optional (revive / double / spin / crate speed). Odds shown for spins & crates.
